# Farm model credits

Last verified: 2026-10-15

The glTF models in this directory are the tractors, animals, barn, hay, corn
and rocks the HarvestBench viewer draws. Each was normalised for the viewer
(feet on y=0, centred, 1.0 tall, textures resized to at most 512 px); the
tractors were also baked to vertex colours and recoloured once per seat. The
sources and their licenses:
